48 hours in Paris

Where to eat, what to skip, and the day trip that's worth the early alarm.

By Léa Martin · 6 min read

Arrive early and the city is yours. Walk the river from the Louvre to the Champ de Mars before the crowds, and you'll have the most famous view in Paris almost to yourself.

Spend the afternoon in the Marais: small galleries, falafel on rue des Rosiers, and a long coffee in the Place des Vosges while the light turns gold.

Got a third morning? Take the train to Mont-Saint-Michel, the tidal island abbey that seems to float above the bay.

GOOD TO KNOW

Most museums close on Mondays or Tuesdays, so check before you plan a day around one. A carnet of metro tickets on your phone saves queueing at every station.
